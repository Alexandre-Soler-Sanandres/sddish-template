# Material scope change

## Prompt and Input State

A planned change acquires new public-contract, schema, security, reversibility, or domain risk.

## Allowed Mutations

Update intent, reclassify lane, revise plan, and request fresh authorization.

## Prohibited Mutations

Continuing under stale classification or approval.

## Minimum Lane

Re-evaluated; never lower than existing lane.

## Durable Artifacts

Updated work package and explicit reclassification record.

## Approval Points

Fresh approval after material scope/risk change.

## Risk Controls

Identify changed boundaries, reversibility, and evidence needs.

## Verification Evidence

Updated acceptance/preservation mapping and reviewer check.

## Expected Stop Paths

Stop until user resolves the new scope or risk.

## Objective Assertions

Material change invalidates stale plan/approval and produces a visible reclassification.
